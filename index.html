About Me | 关于我

I am Jiayue Yang, a PhD student in Applied Mathematics at the University of Waterloo, Canada, and an Associate Graduate Student at both the Perimeter Institute for Theoretical Physics (PI) and the Institute for Quantum Computing (IQC). My research focuses on the intersection of quantum information and quantum gravity.

🎓 Education

  • Ph.D. Student (Sep 2024 - Aug 2028) - University of Waterloo, Applied Math, Full Scholarship 50,800 CAD×4 years, Average Score 96.33/100
  • Master's (Sep 2022 - Aug 2024) - University of Waterloo, Physics, Full Scholarship 44,740 CAD×2 years, Average Score 94.33/100
  • Bachelor's (Sep 2018 - Jun 2022) - Tang Aoqing Honors Program, Jilin University, Physics, Average Score 91.16/100

🔬 Research Interests

  • High Energy Physics - Theory(HEP-TH)
  • General Relativity and Quantum Cosmology(GRQC)
  • Black Hole Thermodynamics(BHT)
  • Quantum Information in Quantum Gravity (QIQG)
  • Relativistic Quantum Information (RQI)
  • Quantum Information and Quantum Computing (QIQC)

🌟 Research Highlights

My research covers a broad range of topics, including black hole thermodynamics, holographic quantum information, relativistic quantum information, wormhole solutions, and analogue black holes.

Harvesting Magic Resource in Curved Spacetime 🎤 Talk 🔗 Poster 📑 Slides

Yang, J., Bhattacharya, D., Zhang, M., Mann, R. B. DOI: arxiv: 2508.16466

我们开发出一套解析级数方法，可用于研究在弯曲时空中的魔力资源收获。

Finite-cutoff Holographic Thermodynamics

Zhang, M., Tan, W., Lu, M., Bhattacharya. D, Yang, J., Mann, R. B. Phys.Rev.Res. 8 (2026) 1, 013208 DOI: 10.1103/f94c-37sk

在有限截断全息框架下建立热力学体系，将 AdS/CFT 推广到有限径向截断与边界的 T^2 变形 CFT，并给出一致的第一定律与能量定义。

Critical Slowing Down of Black Hole Phase Transition and Kinetic Crossover in Supercritical Regime

Li, R., Zhang, K., Yang, J., Mann, R. B., Wang, J. Physical Review D 112 (2025) 6, 064004. DOI: 10.1103/z8bq-kvq3

研究 RN–AdS 黑洞在超临界点附近的动力学，揭示显著的“临界减速”；在超临界区识别出动力学 Widom 线，与热力学 Widom 线高度一致。

On Thermodynamics of Charged and Accelerating Black Holes

Hale, T., Kubizňák, D., Menšíková, J., Mann, R. B., Yang, J.. Physical Review D 111 (2025) 104004. DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevD.111.104004

关于带电加速 AdS 黑洞热力学的综述，澄清规范化与配分函数等关键问题。

Smart Holes: Analogue Black Holes with the Right Temperature and Entropy 🎤 Talk 🔗 Poster

Yang, J., Afshordi, N., Torabian, M., Jafari, S. A., Baskaran, G. Journal of High Energy Physics (JHEP) 07 (2025) 226. DOI: 10.1007/JHEP07(2025)226

提出“智能洞”类比黑洞，可同时模拟真实黑洞的温度与熵。

Existence of Vacuum Wormholes in Einsteinian Cubic Gravity 📑 Slides

Lu, M., Yang, J., Mann, R. B. Journal of High Energy Physics (JHEP) 03 (2025) 073. DOI: 10.1007/JHEP03(2025)073

在爱因斯坦立方引力中构造无需外来物质的真空虫洞解，并验证可穿越性。

Gravitational Wormholes 📑 Slides

Lu, M., Yang, J., Mann, R. B. Universe 10(6), 257 (2024). DOI: 10.3390/universe10060257

邀稿：关于纯引力虫洞的研究，尤其是高阶曲率理论中的可穿越虫洞。

Complexity, Scaling, and a Phase Transition 🎤 Talk 🔗 Poster 📑 Slides

Yang, J., Frey, A. R. Journal of High Energy Physics (JHEP) 09 (2023) 029. DOI: 10.1007/JHEP09(2023)029

研究圆上紧化 CFT 的全息复杂度，发现复杂度在相变中可作序参量，并满足标度律。

Dynamic Behaviours of Black Hole Phase Transitions Near Quadruple Points 🎤 Talk 📑 Slides

Yang, J., Mann, R. B. Journal of High Energy Physics (JHEP) 08 (2023) 028. DOI: 10.1007/JHEP08(2023)028

首次研究黑洞四重点附近的相变动力学，揭示弱/强振荡等丰富现象。

Research | 研究

Probing holographic conformal field theories 🎤 Talk 🔗 Poster 📑 Slides

Zhang, M., Yang, J., Bhattacharya, D., Mann, R. B. DOI: arxiv: 2602.07895

We sugguest a viable way of testing holography principle through quantum information resource.

Harvesting Magic Resource in Curved Spacetime 🎤 Talk 🔗 Poster 📑 Slides

Yang, J., Bhattacharya, D., Zhang, M., Mann, R. B. DOI: arxiv: 2508.16466

We develop analytic tools for analytically investigating the harvesting of non-stabilizerness (magic resources) in anti-de Sitter spacetime of arbitrary dimension.

Finite-cutoff Holographic Thermodynamics

Zhang, M., Tan, W., Lu, M., Bhattacharya. D, Yang, J., Mann, R. B. Phys.Rev.Res. 8 (2026) 1, 013208 DOI: 10.1103/f94c-37sk

Develops a framework for holographic thermodynamics in finite-cutoff holography, coupling a finite radial cutoff in AdS to a boundary T^2-deformed CFT and formulating consistent first laws/energetics.

Critical Slowing Down of Black Hole Phase Transition and Kinetic Crossover in Supercritical Regime

Li, R., Zhang, K., Yang, J., Mann, R. B., Wang, J. Physical Review D 112 (2025) 6, 064004. DOI: 10.1103/z8bq-kvq3

Shows pronounced critical slowing down near spinodal/critical points in RN–AdS black holes and identifies a kinetic Widom line in the supercritical regime matching the thermodynamic one.

On Thermodynamics of Charged and Accelerating Black Holes

Hale, T., Kubizňák, D., Menšíková, J., Mann, R. B., Yang, J.. Physical Review D 111 (2025) 104004. DOI: 10.1103/PhysRevD.111.104004

Revisits the thermodynamics of charged slowly accelerating AdS black holes, clarifying key normalization and action issues.

Smart Holes: Analogue Black Holes with the Right Temperature and Entropy 🎤 Talk 🔗 Poster

Yang, J., Afshordi, N., Torabian, M., Jafari, S. A., Baskaran, G. Journal of High Energy Physics (JHEP) 07 (2025) 226. DOI: 10.1007/JHEP07(2025)226

Proposes “smart holes,” analogue black holes that match both temperature and entropy of GR black holes.

Existence of Vacuum Wormholes in Einsteinian Cubic Gravity 📑 Slides

Lu, M., Yang, J., Mann, R. B. Journal of High Energy Physics (JHEP) 03 (2025) 073. DOI: 10.1007/JHEP03(2025)073

Discovers vacuum wormhole solutions in Einsteinian Cubic Gravity without exotic matter and verifies traversability.

Gravitational Wormholes 📑 Slides

Lu, M., Yang, J., Mann, R. B. Universe 10(6), 257 (2024). DOI: 10.3390/universe10060257

Invited paper for gravitational wormholes, with emphasis on higher-curvature theories.

Complexity, Scaling, and a Phase Transition 🎤 Talk 🔗 Poster 📑 Slides

Yang, J., Frey, A. R. Journal of High Energy Physics (JHEP) 09 (2023) 029. DOI: 10.1007/JHEP09(2023)029

Shows that holographic complexity on a circle acts as an order parameter and obeys a scaling relation.

Dynamic Behaviours of Black Hole Phase Transitions Near Quadruple Points 🎤 Talk 📑 Slides

Yang, J., Mann, R. B. Journal of High Energy Physics (JHEP) 08 (2023) 028. DOI: 10.1007/JHEP08(2023)028

First study of dynamics near black hole quadruple points, revealing rich oscillatory phenomena.
